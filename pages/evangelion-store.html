Day 4

Evangelion Store TOKYO-01

The only one in Tokyo

The official Evangelion store, moved up from Harajuku to the sixth floor of Ikebukuro PARCO. It is the only permanent Eva shop in the city: displays, apparel, model kits, and limited runs that rotate constantly. No ticket, no line, just walk in.

Things to do

01RADIO EVA apparel

The fashion line built on the series art, jackets and tees you cannot get outside Japan.

02Plug-suit collabs

Rotating collaboration drops, often limited to this one store.

03Model kits & figures

Unit-01 through the whole roster, from cheap shelf kits to serious builds.

04Store displays

Life-size props and series art through the shop, worth the walk even if you buy nothing.

05Limited-run goods

Stock turns over constantly, so what is here in August will not be here later.

Good to know

  • Ikebukuro PARCO main building, 6th floor.
  • Open 11:00 to 21:00.
  • It moved here from Harajuku, so old guides send you to the wrong place.
  • Animate's flagship is a few minutes away on foot, do both in one loop.
